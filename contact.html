CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

Answers to common questions regarding enterprise implementation, BAA execution, and EHR integration.

How long does a full Epic or Cerner EHR integration take?

Standard SMART on FHIR v4 integration typically takes 7 to 14 business days. Our clinical engineering team coordinates directly with your hospital IT department to map ADT/ORU message flows and conduct sandbox verification with zero downtime.

What is your guaranteed response SLA for acute care ICU telemetry?

Our Tier-1 Emergency Helpdesk guarantees a phone response in under 5 minutes for inpatient telemetry anomalies. Enterprise hospital contracts also include 99.999% fault-tolerant cloud availability guarantees.

Do you execute custom Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)?

Yes, every institutional engagement includes a mutually executed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA). We guarantee 256-bit zero-knowledge encryption for all protected health information (PHI) at rest and in transit.

Can Pulse Health be deployed on-premise or in dedicated private clouds?

Yes. While most hospitals utilize our certified isolated AWS/GCP HIPAA cloud tenancy, we also provide containerized on-premise edge appliances that process neural inferences entirely behind your hospital firewall.

How does your ambient voice scribe handle multi-lingual clinical visits?

Our ambient NLP models natively support over 40 languages and medical dialects, seamlessly translating and generating structured English SOAP notes and ICD-10 billing codes directly in the electronic chart.
